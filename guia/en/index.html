The 3 steps, with real screenshots

How to claim your profile, screen by screen

These are screenshots from our registration, in Portuguese, just as they appeared to us.

1

Find your business

Go to chatgpt.com/business-hub (or open the ChatGPT sidebar) and search for your business. Try the short name and the full name. Found yours? Choose the listing with your address. If you can’t find it, click Create.

2

Prove the business is yours

First, it asks how you serve customers. Physical location (shop, clinic, office): proof is usually a code sent to your business phone. Online store: it asks for your website and a line to add to your site’s DNS, the “address book” for your domain. Whoever manages your website can do it in a minute, or the agent can do it for you.

3

Tell them who you are

Choose a category from a list, write a description of up to 300 characters, and add delivery and return details. Write it the way you want ChatGPT to describe you. Then verify your identity with an ID and selfie. Without that, your profile stays a draft. After that, invite your team.

Tips

What we learned by doing it ourselves

We learned all of this during our own registration. The full kit has 19 tips.

🔎 Search both names

Try the short name and the full name. A name match is the most common problem: “INEMA” brought up another business.

🏢 Use the business account

The person who claims the profile becomes its owner. Don’t use an employee’s personal account; they might leave.

✂️ 300 characters, no warning

The description gets cut off mid-word. Count first. The kit includes a character counter.

⏳ The DNS record expires

After about 24 hours. Add it and verify it the same day. Don’t delete it afterward.

📋 Choose from the category list

Search by keyword. There’s no “course” category for online courses, so we chose “AI Business.”

📞 Phone and email are public

They’re optional. Only add contact details you’re comfortable sharing with customers.

↩️ Returns in Brazil

Online purchases already come with a 7-day cooling-off period. We selected “free, 7 days.”

🧩 Extensions can get in the way

A browser extension popped up over the page and captured keystrokes. Close your extensions or use a clean browser window.

🪪 No identity check, no published profile

Your ID and selfie unlock the dashboard, the business agent, and full editing access.

Common questions

Before you get started

Does it cost anything?

No. Claiming your profile is free, and we used a free ChatGPT account. Paid options (ads and the Business plan) are available, but you don’t need them.

Does it work in Brazil?

It worked for us in Portuguese on 11/22/2026. It’s in beta: if you can’t access it yet, the kit will be useful when it becomes available to you.

Do I need to code?

No. The 3 steps involve clicking and filling in a form. Whoever manages your website can add the DNS record, or the agent can do it if you say yes.

What if someone has already claimed my business?

Sources mention an option to dispute ownership and support for locked profiles. We haven’t encountered this ourselves. Let us know if it happens to you.

Will this put me at the top of the answers?

There’s no promise of a particular ranking. The benefit is that ChatGPT has accurate, verified information about your business, and you can see how it describes you.

Can the agent make a mistake?

Yes. That’s why it stops before each sensitive step and shows you what it’s about to do. You review the description before it’s submitted.
